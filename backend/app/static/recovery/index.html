Recovery plan

From discharge orders to a daily schedule

Provide the instructions your doctor gave you. RecoveryAI only organises what is written. It never adds medications, doses, durations or activities, and anything missing stays Not specified.

Educational research prototype trained on de-identified records of US inpatients with diabetes (UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals, 1999–2008). Not medical advice, and never a clinical discharge decision.
